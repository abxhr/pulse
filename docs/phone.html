On your phone

Pulse is a progressive web app. Install it to your Home Screen and it opens full screen with its own icon.

At home (same Wi-Fi)

With HOST=0.0.0.0 (the default in .env.example) open http://<your-computer's-ip>:8080 on your phone. The password you set protects it.

From anywhere: Tailscale

Tailscale puts your phone and your computer on one private network. No ports are opened and nothing is public.

  1. Install Tailscale on the computer and on your phone and sign in to the same account.
  2. On the computer run tailscale serve --bg 8080 once. You get a private HTTPS address like https://<device>.<tailnet>.ts.net.
  3. Add that address to CORS_ORIGINS in .env and restart Pulse.
  4. Open it on your phone and choose Add to Home Screen (iOS Safari: Share, then Add to Home Screen). HTTPS is what makes the install and offline shell work.

The computer has to be on and running Pulse. See Set up for starting it automatically.
